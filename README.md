# GreenLoop

GreenLoop is a circular agriculture platform for Mekong Delta farming systems. It connects farmers, cooperatives, processors, buyers and administrators around biomass collection, logistics, biochar/bio-material processing, dMRV evidence, carbon credits, reward points and green finance readiness.

## What Is Covered

- Biomass intake for rice straw, rice husk, aquaculture sludge, seafood by-products, melaleuca/cajeput residue, coconut husk, coffee husk and mixed biomass.
- HTX collection booking, pickup management, logistics routes and workflow tasks.
- Biochar and bio-material output tracking with custody hashes.
- dMRV carbon passport evidence packages with source, mass, custody, soil, verification and marketplace checks.
- IoT-style live readings for salinity, pH, dissolved oxygen, temperature, organic matter and moisture.
- Carbon records, verification, issuance, marketplace offers and buyer interest requests.
- Residue source records, conversion pathways, logistics assignments, field-return applications and ESG certificates.
- Farmer reward points and redemption vouchers.
- Rice - aquaculture - melaleuca ecosystem profiling and green finance applications.
