How it works

End-to-end from field
to carbon market

GreenLoop unifies biomass logistics, partner pyrolysis, digital MRV, Soil-Water intelligence, and ASEAN carbon trading within a single farmer-facing platform.

Biomass Collection
HTX cooperatives serve as hub-and-spoke micro-collection points. Min 500 kg/pickup, cost kept below $12/tonne.
Partner Pyrolysis
Asset-light model: HUSK Vietnam and Biocare convert residues into EBC-certified biochar at ~35% mass yield.
Automated MRV
Three-tier VM0044 verification with digital custody chain shortens credit registration to 6–9 months.
Water Intelligence
MRC salinity API overlay fires season-switch alerts when intrusion crosses 5 g/L. Pond DO monitoring in Phase 2.
Carbon Passport
Blockchain-anchored credential unlocks green loans, preferential insurance, and direct AICE exchange listing.
AICE Trading
Credits settle as ASEAN Standard Carbon Units on the AICE interoperable exchange. Real-time SCU oracle pricing.

Frequently Asked Questions
How is biochar measured in CO₂e?
GreenLoop applies the Verra VM0044 methodology with a validated pyrolysis efficiency of ~35% mass yield. Each tonne of biochar sequesters approximately 2.5 tCO₂e (rice straw). The Carbon Engine calculates exact values per feedstock type and batch weight.
When do I receive carbon credit payments?
After biomass is delivered and processed, GreenLoop nests into Biocare's existing certified project, reducing verification time from 18 months to 6–9 months. Payout is then released directly to your AICE wallet via smart contract after successful AICE registry confirmation.
At what salinity should I switch to shrimp season?
The Salinity Season Scheduler fires an alert when intrusion crosses 5 g/L (shrimp optimal threshold). Rice cultivation is safe below 1 g/L. The 1–5 g/L range is the transition window - GreenLoop recommends switching 5 days before the forecast 5 g/L crossing date.
Is shrimp pond sludge eligible for credits?
Yes - GreenLoop's dual feedstock model co-processes pond sludge alongside rice straw. Shrimp sludge biochar yields ~18% mass with 1.5 tCO₂e per tonne biochar (B-Grade). Sludge diversion from canals also creates a measurable water-quality co-benefit within the VM0044 project boundary.
How do new households enroll?
New households enroll through their local HTX cooperative. The cooperative staff acts as a Tier 1 MRV verifier - digitally signing biomass inputs at the collection point. No smartphone or individual account is required; the cooperative's sign-off creates the initial Carbon Farmer Passport record.
